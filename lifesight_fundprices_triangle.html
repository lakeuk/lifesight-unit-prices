ℹ️ About LifeSight Return Matrix
How to Use the App
  • Load CSV dataset: Drag and drop the CSV file or click the browse button to select it.
  • Select a Fund: Choose a single fund tile at the bottom to load its return matrix.
  • Display Mode: Switch between percentage returns (%) or growth of a £1,000 investment (£).
  • Preset Timeframes: Click 1Y, 3Y, or 5Y for instant date range adjustments.
  • Inflation Adjustment: Move the Inflation Rate slider to recalculate real returns against purchasing power.
  • Market Events: Hover over cells (marked with a gold dot) to see major market context markers (e.g. COVID drop).
⚠️ Disclaimer: Data points represent fund prices at a single point in the month and do not reflect the exact day of the month a member's contribution is executed by the administrator.
