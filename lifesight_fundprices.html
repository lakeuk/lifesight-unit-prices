ℹ️ About LifeSight Fund Analytics Dashboard
How to Use the Dashboard
  • Load Data: Drag & drop lifesight_fundprices.csv or click Browse File.
  • Toggle View: Click the chart mode button to switch between Unit Price Chart and Percent Change Chart.
  • Select Funds: Filter funds by Administrator or Search term, then click fund tiles to toggle active display.
  • Adjust Timeframes: Drag the dual slider range, select preset ranges (1Y, 3Y, 5Y, MAX), or enter exact dates manually.
  • Benchmark Target: Overlay fixed compound return targets (e.g. +5% p.a.) against fund performance.
  • Export Analytics: Use the Export Data dropdown to export filtered dataset to CSV, Excel, or PNG chart images.
Risk & Performance Metrics Definitions
  • Total Return: Overall percentage performance across the selected date window.
  • CAGR: Compound Annual Growth Rate over the selected duration.
  • Ann. Volatility: Annualized standard deviation of daily returns representing risk/price fluctuation.
  • Max Drawdown: The largest peak-to-trough decline over the period.
  • Sharpe Ratio (Rf=2%): Risk-adjusted return measure calculated above a 2.0% risk-free rate.
⚠️ Note: All values are calculated from historical unit price data and are for analytical comparison purposes.
